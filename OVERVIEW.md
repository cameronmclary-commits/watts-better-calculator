# Watts Better — Solar & Battery Energy Advisor

A public-facing energy analysis tool for homeowners. Customers enter their electricity usage details (tariff, rates, solar info), see interactive 24-hour usage/cost visualisations with TOU breakdowns, model solar and battery scenarios with live sliders, then submit their details to generate a personalised savings report and create a CRM lead.

**Who it's for:** Homeowners exploring solar and battery options. No login required — publicly accessible.

**Key features:**
- Manual entry of tariff & usage details (flat rate or time-of-use)
- 24-hour energy profile visualisation with colour-coded TOU charts
- Solar production overlay showing self-consumed vs exported energy
- Interactive scenario modelling with solar size slider and battery capacity selector
- Lead capture form that saves to the Leads database table for CRM follow-up
- Embedded battery comparison step (external comparison app) before the report
- On-screen personalised report (printable / save as PDF); emailing the report is planned once an email account is connected
